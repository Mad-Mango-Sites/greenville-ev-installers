What to know about charging in Taylors

One permit office. Since Taylors is unincorporated, every permit goes through Greenville County's eTRAKiT system. The contractor files it, and the county schedules the inspection.

Local shops. Viper Services, Touchstone Electric, Teleios Plumbing & Electrical and Jaymarc Electric all list Taylors addresses, and Mr. Electric's BBB profile lists Taylors. A shop close by can make it easier to get someone out for the site visit and the inspection.

Mixed housing. Taylors has older homes near the old mill and newer subdivisions farther out. The older homes are the ones most likely to need panel work.

Taylors FAQ

Who inspects an EV charger install in Taylors?

Greenville County Building Safety. Your installer requests the inspection after the work is done.

Do I need the permit for the Duke credit?

Yes. Duke asks for a copy of the approved permit from the local permitting office with every credit application.
