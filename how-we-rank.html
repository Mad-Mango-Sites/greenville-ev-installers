How We Rank Installers

Every installer on this site gets a score out of 100. Here's exactly how we calculate it, so you can decide how much weight to give it.

The five factors

FactorWeightHow we score it
EV specialization30 points30 if the installer has a dedicated EV page and names charger brands or shows EV jobs or EV-specific reviews. 22 if it has a dedicated EV page or shows EV projects without that detail. 15 if EV charging is listed among general services.
Rating, adjusted for review volume30 pointsWe blend the installer's average rating with a neutral 4.7, weighted by how many reviews it has. A handful of 5-star reviews counts for less than hundreds of 4.9s. Google ratings come first. If we can't confirm a Google rating, we use a named review platform such as Thumbtack or HomeAdvisor. Ratings shown only on the installer's own website aren't scored until confirmed.
Verification20 pointsLicense confirmed on the SC LLR lookup (10), insurance confirmed (5), and the installer states it pulls permits (5). Until our LLR checks are done, a license number the installer publishes earns 5 points.
Town coverage10 pointsOn a town page: 10 if the installer names that town as a service area, 5 if it only says it covers the wider area. On site-wide lists: the share of our 8 towns it names.
Transparency10 points10 if the installer publishes prices or a pricing guide. Warranty terms will count here once installers share them.

What doesn't affect the score

  • Paying us. Sponsored spots, if we offer them, appear in a labeled box and are never numbered as part of the ranking.
  • Whether an installer has claimed its listing.

Where our data comes from

We checked each installer's own website, public review listings and business directories by hand in October 2026. Each profile shows its sources and the date it was last checked. Ratings change, so we recheck every quarter.

Who we leave out

  • National lead networks that use toll-free numbers and templated city pages
  • Businesses whose websites are down or redirect elsewhere
  • Electricians with no sign of EV work
  • Businesses we can't verify are active
