Priority Electrical Service, formerly Upstate Electrical Solutions, works from Woodruff Road in Greenville and has a dedicated EV charger page. It installs Tesla Wall Connector, Wallbox, ClipperCreek and JuiceBox chargers, handles panel upgrades and permits, and also repairs EV chargers. It says it has installed hundreds of chargers. It serves homeowners only, so businesses should look elsewhere.

Questions to ask when you call

  • Can you do a load calculation before quoting?
  • Will you pull the permit and be there for the inspection?
  • Can you list labor and materials separately on the invoice? (Duke requires it for the Charger Prep Credit.)
  • Do you set up and commission the Tesla Wall Connector, or just run the circuit?
