Enhanced Electric

Greenville

Dedicated EV page with named chargers or documented EV jobs
76/100
Our score

Enhanced Electric, formerly Enhanced Lighting, is a Greenville company that publishes its EV install prices, which few installers do: $750 to $1,500 for a Tesla Wall Connector (Gen 3\) and $500 to $900 for a NEMA 14-50 outlet, as listed in Oct 2026. It also installs ChargePoint, JuiceBox and Grizzl-E chargers, sub-panels and multi-EV setups. It doesn't publish a street address.

Questions to ask when you call

  • Can you do a load calculation before quoting?
  • Will you pull the permit and be there for the inspection?
  • Can you list labor and materials separately on the invoice? (Duke requires it for the Charger Prep Credit.)
  • Do you set up and commission the Tesla Wall Connector, or just run the circuit?
