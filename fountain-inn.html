What to know about charging in Fountain Inn

Pull the permit first. Fountain Inn's residential permit page says permit fees double when work starts before a permit is issued. A licensed installer will file it before starting.

Ford Lightning and big trucks. Some installers here have done high-amp setups for trucks. One More Hand mentions a 90-amp Ford Lightning install on its site. If you have a truck with home backup features, say so up front, since that changes the equipment.

Co-op or Duke. As in Simpsonville and Mauldin, some streets are on Laurens Electric. Duke's credit only applies to Duke customers.

Fountain Inn FAQ

Is Fountain Inn in Greenville County?

Mostly. Part of the city reaches into Laurens County. Inside city limits, the City of Fountain Inn issues permits either way.

My new home has a garage outlet already. Is it a charger outlet?

Maybe not. Builders sometimes add a 240-volt outlet or a pre-wired circuit, and sometimes just a standard 120-volt outlet. Have an electrician check the breaker size and outlet type before you buy a charger.
