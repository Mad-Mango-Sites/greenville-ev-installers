What about "Tesla Certified" installers?

Tesla keeps its own list of certified installers on its website. Jaymarc Electric says on its site that it is a certified Tesla Wall Connector installer. That's the installer's own claim, so ask to see its Tesla certification when you call. Any licensed electrician can install a Wall Connector. Certification mainly matters if you want to buy through Tesla's own install program.

What a Wall Connector install usually involves

  • A dedicated 240-volt circuit. The Wall Connector can charge at up to 48 amps on a 60-amp breaker. Many homes use a lower setting on a 40- or 50-amp circuit to avoid a panel upgrade.
  • A load calculation. The installer adds up your home's existing load to see how much room is left on your panel.
  • Wi-Fi setup and commissioning. The charger is set to match the breaker using Tesla's app.
  • A permit and inspection from your city or Greenville County.

What it costs

Enhanced Electric publishes $750 to $1,500 to install a Tesla Wall Connector (Gen 3), as listed on its site in Oct 2026. Other installers quote after a site visit or photos. Panel work is extra. See the full cost guide.
