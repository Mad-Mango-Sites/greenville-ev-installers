Duke credit for businesses

Duke Energy Carolinas also offers the Charger Prep Credit to non-residential customers in South Carolina. Business customers apply directly. There's no contractor discount option, and the application needs a Customer Usage Profile form. The tariff caps the credit per charger by type. For example, a public Level 2 charger is capped at $1,054 and a workplace Level 2 charger at $793 (amounts based on rates effective April 1, 2023). See the Duke credit guide for details.

Questions to ask a commercial installer

  • Have you done a project like this one (workplace, retail, fleet or DC fast)? Can I see one?
  • Will you handle the utility coordination and the load study?
  • Which charger hardware and networks do you work with?
  • Who handles warranty and service calls after it's installed?
