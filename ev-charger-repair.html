EV Charger Repair in Greenville, SC

If your charger stops working, the problem is often the circuit, not the charger: a tripped breaker, a loose connection or a worn outlet. A licensed electrician can test the circuit and tell you whether to call the charger maker under warranty.

What to check before you call

  1. Is the breaker for the charger tripped?
  2. Does the charger's app or light show a fault code? Write it down.
  3. For a plug-in charger, is the plug or outlet warm, scorched or loose? If so, stop using it and call an electrician.
  4. Is the charger still under the maker's warranty? Many brands want you to contact them first.
