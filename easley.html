Easley at a glance

Electric utilityEasley Combined Utilities serves most of the city. Addresses outside ECU's area may be Duke Energy Carolinas or Blue Ridge Electric Cooperative.
Duke Charger Prep CreditOnly for Duke customers. ECU customers aren't eligible for Duke's program.
Permit officeInside city limits: City of Easley Building Department, 205 N. First St. (residential permits can be applied for online). Outside: Pickens County or Anderson County, depending on the parcel.
CountyPickens County (a small part of Easley extends into Anderson County)

What to know about charging in Easley

ECU, not Duke. If your bill says Easley Combined Utilities, contact ECU about EV rates before you buy a charger. ECU also runs a load management program, so ask whether it affects when you should charge.

Not Greenville County. Permit rules come from the City of Easley or Pickens County, not Greenville County. Installers that work across the Upstate know both.

Easley FAQ

Is the Duke credit available in Easley?

Only if your power bill is from Duke Energy. Most homes in the city are on Easley Combined Utilities.

Do Greenville installers work in Easley?

Some do. The ones marked "Names Easley" list it on their own websites. Others cover the whole Upstate; confirm your address when you contact them.
