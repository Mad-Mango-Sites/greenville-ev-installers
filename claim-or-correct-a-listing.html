Claim or Correct a Listing

Basic listings are free. If you own one of the businesses on this site, you can claim your profile to add details and fix anything we got wrong. Homeowners can email us the same way to report an error.

What you can add when you claim

  • Your SC license number (we'll verify it on LLR)
  • Towns you serve
  • Charger brands you install
  • Your Duke Energy approved-contractor status for the Charger Prep Credit
  • Which of these you do: multifamily or HOA projects, commercial projects, DC fast charging, charger repair
  • Published prices or a price range
  • Warranty terms
  • Photos of your EV work (that you own the rights to)

What happens next

We'll confirm you work for the business by calling the number on your own website or emailing your business address. Then we update the profile, usually within a few business days. Claiming is free and doesn't change your ranking.

Want to be removed?

Email us and we'll review it. We may keep basic public facts (name, phone, website) if the business is active and serves the area, but we'll correct anything inaccurate.
