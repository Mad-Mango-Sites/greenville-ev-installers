What Does EV Charger Installation Cost in Greenville, SC?

Two neighbors can get quotes that are hundreds of dollars apart for what sounds like the same job. The difference is almost always in the house, not the charger. Here's what moves the price, and what one local installer actually charges.

Published local prices

Most installers quote after seeing the site. Enhanced Electric is the only Greenville-area installer we found that publishes EV prices on its website (as listed in Oct 2026):

JobPublished price
Install a Tesla Wall Connector (Gen 3\)$750 to $1,500
Install a NEMA 14-50 outlet$500 to $900

Greenville Electric LLC also has a pricing guide on its site. Treat any published range as a starting point. Your quote depends on the things below.

The five things that set your price

1. Distance from the panel

A charger on the other side of the garage wall from the panel is the cheapest job. Every extra foot means more wire and conduit. Running to a detached garage usually means a trench.

2. Panel capacity

If your panel has room, the job is a new breaker and circuit. If it doesn't, the options run from a sub-panel or load-management device up to a full service upgrade, which is the biggest jump in price.

3. Charger speed

A higher-amp charger needs a bigger breaker and heavier wire. Many homes do fine with a 40-amp charging setting, which covers most daily driving overnight.

4. Hardwired or plug-in

A plug-in charger needs a NEMA 14-50 outlet. A hardwired charger connects directly. Prices are similar; the choice depends on the charger and where it's mounted.

5. Permit and inspection

Every install needs a permit from your city or Greenville County. The installer usually includes the fee in the quote. Ask.

Ways to lower the cost

  • Duke's Charger Prep Credit, if you're a Duke customer: up to $1,236 for most Upstate homes, toward wiring, conduit, the outlet and panel work. Not the charger itself.
  • Put the charger close to the panel if you can.
  • Ask about a lower amperage setting before you agree to a panel upgrade.
  • Get more than one quote. It's the easiest way to see whether a panel upgrade is really needed.
