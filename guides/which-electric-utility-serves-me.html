Which Electric Utility Serves My Address?

Greenville County has more than one power company. Which one you have decides whether you can get Duke's Charger Prep Credit and which EV rates you can ask about. The quickest way to know is the name on your power bill.

The utilities in our area

UtilityWhereDuke Charger Prep Credit?
Duke Energy CarolinasMost of Greenville County and the UpstateYes
Greer CPWMostly inside Greer city limits, plus some nearby unincorporated areasNo
Easley Combined UtilitiesThe City of EasleyNo
Blue Ridge Electric CooperativeParts of northern Greenville County, Pickens, Oconee, Anderson and Spartanburg counties. District office on Highway 11 near Travelers RestNo
Laurens Electric CooperativeParts of southern Greenville County and six other counties. District office on East Butler Road near MauldinNo

How to check your address

  • Look at your bill. The company name is at the top.
  • Greer: Greer CPW has a service availability map on its website.
  • Moving soon? Ask the seller or landlord for a recent bill, or call the utilities above with the address.

What changes depending on your utility

  • Duke customers: you can apply for the Charger Prep Credit.
  • Everyone else: call your utility and ask about EV rates, rebates or load-management programs before you buy a charger. Easley Combined Utilities, for example, runs a load management program. We only list programs we've confirmed.
  • Panel upgrades: whoever your utility is, they'll need to schedule the disconnect and reconnect if your service is being upgraded.
