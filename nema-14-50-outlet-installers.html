NEMA 14-50 Outlet Installers in Greenville, SC

A NEMA 14-50 is a 240-volt, 50-amp outlet. It's the plug most portable EV chargers and many plug-in wall chargers use. It's a good choice if you want to take the charger with you when you move, or if you're not sure which charger you'll end up with.

Outlet or hardwired?

  • Outlet (NEMA 14-50): easy to swap chargers, portable. A plug-in charger on a 50-amp circuit typically charges at up to 40 amps. Use a heavy-duty, industrial-grade receptacle; cheap ones can overheat under hours of EV charging.
  • Hardwired: allows higher charging speeds on a bigger circuit, no plug to wear out, and it's the better fit outdoors.

What it costs

Enhanced Electric publishes $500 to $900 for a NEMA 14-50 install, as listed on its site in Oct 2026. Long wire runs and panel work cost more.
