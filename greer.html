Greer at a glance

Electric utilityGreer CPW serves most homes inside the city and some nearby unincorporated areas (check CPW's service availability map). Other addresses are mostly Duke Energy Carolinas.
Duke Charger Prep CreditOnly if your bill is from Duke Energy. Greer CPW customers aren't eligible for Duke's program.
Permit officeInside city limits: City of Greer Planning and Development (permits and inspections). Outside city limits: the county you live in.
CountyGreer sits in both Greenville County and Spartanburg County.

What to know about charging in Greer

Check your bill first. If it says Greer CPW, skip the Duke credit paperwork and ask CPW directly about any EV rates or programs. We only list programs we've confirmed, and we didn't find a CPW EV rebate to list as of Oct 2026.

Two counties. Greer straddles the Greenville and Spartanburg county line. Inside city limits, the City of Greer handles permits. Outside, it's Greenville County or Spartanburg County depending on the parcel. Installers based in Greer deal with all three offices regularly.

Newer subdivisions. Greer has grown steadily for years (Greer CPW reports customer growth averaging about 4% a year over the past decade). Newer homes often have 200-amp service and an attached garage close to the panel, which tends to make for simpler, cheaper installs. Still, ask for a load calculation if you also have electric heat, a heat pump and an electric water heater.

Greer FAQ

Can I get the Duke credit if I'm on Greer CPW?

No. The Charger Prep Credit is a Duke Energy program for Duke customers. Check the name on your power bill.

Who issues the permit in Greer?

Inside city limits, the City of Greer. Its inspections page lists the 2020 National Electrical Code as the code it enforces. Outside city limits, it's Greenville County or Spartanburg County.
